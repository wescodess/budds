# Keep Learn Anything V2 authority and bounded continuations in Convex

Convex will own authenticated V2 state, lifecycle transitions, optimistic revisions, idempotency, quota reservations, leases, checkpoints, and terminal outcomes. Pure modules will own deterministic mastery, feasibility, redaction, and admission decisions. A Convex action, Nitro server route, or Cloudflare Worker may perform approved external fetch and model calls, but it cannot authoritatively advance state without a Convex command. Human approval and learner activity end a bounded job at a durable state; neither keeps a workflow open. This reuses Budds' proven server-authority and job patterns without creating V2 routes, jobs, or tables before their owning implementation tickets.
